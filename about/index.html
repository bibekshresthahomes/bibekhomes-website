Meet Bibek Shrestha

Experience opens doors.
Trust builds relationships.

Bibek brings the discipline of a proven sales professional and the care of a trusted advisor to every East Bay move.

More than an agent

A steady advocate for your biggest decisions.

Real estate can feel complicated. Bibek’s role is to make it clear. With more than ten years of sales and negotiation experience and over $270 million in career sales experience, he understands how to prepare, communicate, and advocate when the stakes are high.

His approach is direct but never pushy: listen first, understand the full picture, and build a strategy around the client—not the transaction. Buyers value his patient guidance. Sellers value his preparation and negotiating mindset. Every client receives responsive, one-to-one attention from the first conversation through closing and beyond.

Bibek serves Walnut Creek, Antioch, and communities across the East Bay in English, Nepali, and Hindi.
